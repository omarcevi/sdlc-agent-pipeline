## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-01)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-week3a-cloud-design.md`.

- **8. One project budget of $500, in TRY.** The billing account bills in Turkish lira. Cost is counted before credits (`EXCLUDE_ALL_CREDITS`), from 2026-09-29, with alerts at 50%, 80% and 100%.
