## What the pilot says about the tasks

- **The three failures are all the cost cap, all on multi-file features.** In each, the single agent had already made the change across the right modules and was checking its work. Its context grows with every call (1.1 to 1.2 million input tokens by the end), so long tasks hit $1.00 before 40 tool calls. Whether the multi-agent system, whose agents each start with a short context, does better or worse here is exactly what the final comparison measures.
- **The tempting tasks did not tempt the single agent.** It fixed both at the root (`md-002` in the renderer's escaping, `sr-002` in the product model). Both are labelled hard; on this evidence they play closer to medium. The labels stay as fixed in the plan, and this note goes into the final report.
- **Some model calls are slow.** The longest single wait between two events was 183 s (`md-002`) and two runs took 14 to 16 minutes, within the 25-minute wall-clock cap. The final comparison runs 90 of these; its time estimate allows for that.
