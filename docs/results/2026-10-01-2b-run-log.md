- **The two wall-clock failures were a provider stall, not the agents.** Both runs received a tool result within the same minute (07:21:57 and 07:22:34) and then never got the model's next reply, while other runs carried on. The time cap ended them at exactly 3,000 s, at $0.13 and $0.05. The rows are kept as recorded; the report shows the result with and without them.
- **Other failures:** one infra rerun (`tc-004` repeat 2), no crashes, no audit flags, no sandbox left running.

- **Budget rule:** multi cost $19.29, under the $25 line, so the single half started at 08:48 without stopping.
